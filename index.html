Projects

Sahi Daam

Verify a weighing instrument's certificate by scanning its QR code.

Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (problem SIH26036, Department of Consumer Affairs) with team VITV SahiDaam; we cleared VIT's internal round. I led the backend, the database and frontend integration.

Each certificate is signed with Ed25519, so an edited record shows as not verified, and an offline checker works with no network. Officers, traders, labs and admins each get their own view across eight States, with fraud checks at issue time and a tamper-evident audit log.

Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL, Render
Source code

NightIT

Order from the hostel night mess and watch your place in the queue.

Built at Hackulus '26. Paper bills were the only proof of purchase, so a lost bill meant lost food. NightIT replaces them with a one-time six-digit pickup code tied to your registration number, and shows your live position from your room.

I built the backend and data layer: a 19-route API, wait times based on the slowest item in a cart, stock limits, cut-off times and UPI payments.

React, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Razorpay
Source code

Misrule

A platformer where every level secretly changes one law of physics.

Built at Code2Create 7.0 with team Hackforgers. Gravity might flip, controls might mirror, time might only pass when you move. You work out which rule is in play, and your score depends on how early you could have known.

JavaScript, no dependencies, works offline

Log what you left on your plate; see what your diet is missing.

Built at NutriHack, Nutrition Week 2026. One log at the tray return tells a student which nutrients they're short on, and tells the mess which dishes are wasted most. Nutrition values come from FSSAI and ICMR-NIN data.
